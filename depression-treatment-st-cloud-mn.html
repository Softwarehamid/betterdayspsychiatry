Depression Treatment in St. Cloud, MN/Central MN/Rural MN

Care for depression and mood concerns.

Better Days Psychiatry provides depression treatment in St. Cloud, MN, Central Minnesota, and rural Minnesota, including psychiatric evaluation, therapy support, and medication management when appropriate.

Depression care with a personalized plan

Depression can affect mood, motivation, sleep, appetite, concentration, relationships, and daily functioning. Treatment begins with a careful conversation about symptoms, history, safety, and goals for care.

Depending on the patient, support may include therapy, medication management, education, lifestyle planning, and ongoing follow-up.

Symptoms worth discussing

  • Persistent sadness, emptiness, irritability, or hopelessness.
  • Loss of interest in activities or relationships.
  • Changes in sleep, appetite, concentration, or energy.
  • Thoughts of self-harm or feeling unsafe, which require immediate help.

Request support

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, call 911 or 988. For non-emergency care, contact the office.
