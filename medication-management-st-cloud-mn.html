Medication Management in St. Cloud, MN/Central MN/Rural MN

Medication support with careful follow-up.

Better Days Psychiatry provides psychiatric medication management in St. Cloud, MN, Central Minnesota, and rural Minnesota for patients who need evaluation, monitoring, refills, and treatment adjustments.

Individualized psychiatric medication management

Medication management is more than writing a prescription. It includes understanding symptoms, reviewing response and side effects, monitoring progress, and adjusting the treatment plan when needed.

Our office supports patients managing anxiety, depression, ADHD, mood disorders, PTSD symptoms, and other mental health concerns. Medication may be used alone or alongside therapy and other support.

What visits may include

  • Review of current symptoms and medication response.
  • Discussion of benefits, side effects, and treatment goals.
  • Refill planning and follow-up recommendations.
  • Coordination with therapy or other services when appropriate.
