FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Better Days Psychiatry provide?

BDP offers addiction treatment, mental health counseling, medication management, and therapy for anxiety, depression, ADHD, PTSD, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and substance use disorders.

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can use the New Patient Form, contact the office by phone, or use the Patient Portal if you are an active patient.

Do I need a referral to see a provider?

No. You may schedule directly through the website or call the office for more information.

What insurance plans do you accept?

BDP accepts many major insurance plans. Patients should confirm their specific coverage and benefits with their plan before care.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive assessment, discussion of symptoms and history, and a personalized treatment plan.

Do you offer telehealth services?

Yes. Telehealth appointments are available for patients who prefer virtual consultation.

What should I do if I need immediate help?

If you are experiencing a crisis, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. You can also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
