Psychiatry in St. Cloud, MN/Central MN

Psychiatric care centered on your goals.

Better Days Psychiatry provides psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, treatment planning, and ongoing mental health support for patients in St. Cloud and Central Minnesota.

Psychiatric evaluations and personalized treatment plans

Psychiatric care begins with understanding your symptoms, history, stressors, strengths, and treatment goals. Our team uses that information to help build a plan that may include therapy, medication management, education, lifestyle support, or referrals when needed.

Patients may seek psychiatric care for anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, PTSD, schizophrenia, substance use concerns, or other mental health conditions. The goal is to provide compassionate care that is practical, evidence-based, and responsive to each person.

When psychiatry may help

  • Symptoms are affecting school, work, relationships, or daily routines.
  • You need diagnostic clarity or a second look at your treatment plan.
  • You are considering psychiatric medication or need medication follow-up.
  • You want coordinated support for both mental health and substance use concerns.
